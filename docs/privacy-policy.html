BuzzOff Privacy Policy

DopplerTechnologies · Effective 29 September 2026

BuzzOff is an alarm clock app for Android made by DopplerTechnologies. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: BuzzOff does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you set up stays on your phone.

Information stored on your device

To work, BuzzOff saves the following only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your alarms: times, repeat days, labels, categories, chosen sound and vibration setting.
  • Your app settings, such as snooze length.
  • Your PIN, stored only as a salted one-way hash, so the PIN itself is never saved.

If Android's device backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your personal device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by DopplerTechnologies.

Information we collect

None. BuzzOff has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

Permissions

PermissionWhy BuzzOff needs it
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)To ring at the exact minute you set.
Notifications and full-screen alertsTo show the ringing alarm, including over the lock screen.
Foreground service (media playback)To keep playing the alarm sound until you snooze or dismiss it.
Vibration and wake lockTo vibrate and keep the phone awake while an alarm rings.
Run at startupTo restore your alarms after the phone restarts.

Children

BuzzOff is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete individual alarms in the app, or uninstall BuzzOff to remove all of its data from your phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
