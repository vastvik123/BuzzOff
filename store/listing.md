## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Set many alarms, wake up once. Silence the rest of them with one PIN.
```

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Do you set five alarms to make sure you wake up, then have to switch every one of them off? BuzzOff fixes that.

Group your alarms into categories like Morning, Nap or Gym. When you're up, tap "I'm up", enter your PIN, and every remaining alarm in that category is silenced for the rest of the day. They come back automatically tomorrow.

WHY BUZZOFF
• One PIN turns off the rest of today's alarms in a category
• Works right from the ringing screen, the moment you wake up
• The PIN stops a half-asleep you from switching everything off by accident
• Changed your mind? "Turn back on" restores them

BACKUP ALARMS IN ONE STEP
Add a series in seconds: first alarm at 6:00, then 3 more every 5 minutes. BuzzOff shows you exactly when each one will ring.

EASY TO SET UP
• Pick the time on a clock dial, or type it on the keyboard
• Repeat once, every day, on weekdays, on weekends or on custom days
• Labels, alarm sounds and vibration for each alarm
• Swipe to delete, with Undo

WAKES YOU UP GENTLY, BUT RELIABLY
• Full-screen alarm, even on the lock screen
• Volume rises gradually over 30 seconds
• Snooze from 1 minute to 60, your choice
• A countdown to your next alarm
• Alarms survive restarts and time-zone changes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection. Your alarms and PIN never leave your phone.

Tip: on some phones (such as OnePlus, Oppo, Xiaomi and Samsung), allow BuzzOff to run in the background so alarms always ring. BuzzOff shows you how on first launch.
```
